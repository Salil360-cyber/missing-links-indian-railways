What should count most?

The opportunity score mixes three parts, each scaled 0–1: demand (bigger cities closer together), speed gap (how far the fastest train is below 90 km/h) and frequency gap (how few direct trains run). The study used 50 / 30 / 20. Move the sliders and the map and table re-rank.

Data: Datameet Indian Railways timetable (around 2016) and Census 2011 city populations; satellite cities are merged into their metro. Pairs to Srinagar had no rail link in 2016, so they need new track rather than a new train and are not ranked.
